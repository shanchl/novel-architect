# Domain Checks

Long-form novels often depend on project-specific systems: law, magic, forensic procedure, technology, logistics, nonhuman embodiment, economics, languages, or social rules. Keep those details inside the novel project, not in this skill.

## Storage

Use existing project files first:

- `02_world/rules.yaml` for hard constraints.
- `02_world/terms.yaml` for named concepts, tools, institutions, measurements, places, and procedures.
- `09_writing/vocabulary.md` for preferred diction and terminology.
- `09_writing/forbidden_patterns.md` for banned wording.
- `09_writing/writing_rules.md` for chapter-level style and craft constraints.

Add focused project files only when repeated checks need them, for example:

- `02_world/domain_rules.yaml`
- `02_world/procedure_windows.yaml`
- `08_memory/information_ledger.yaml`
- `08_memory/scene_clock.yaml`
- `09_writing/term_guardrails.yaml`

## Procedure and Evidence

When a chapter uses professional, scientific, legal, magical, procedural, or technical evidence, separate:

- Phenomenon observed.
- Source of the observation.
- Time or condition window.
- Method used.
- Error range or uncertainty.
- Conclusion allowed.
- Conclusion not allowed.

Do not fuse separate evidence streams into one conclusion. If two facts come from different methods or time bases, state them separately.

## Measurement and Infrastructure

Any precise measurement, timestamp, map, record, report, facility, device, spell, biological process, or institution must have an in-world source.

When the source is missing:

- Add the term or rule to project canon.
- Lower the precision.
- Replace the measurement with a project-plausible proxy.
- Or remove the claim.

## Terminology

Terminology checks should distinguish:

- Literal body-part or domain terms that must follow project canon.
- Idioms that may be intentionally retained.
- Character voice exceptions.
- Narration exceptions.

Do not mechanically replace every match. Record allowed exceptions in a project file so later checks can reproduce the decision.

## Trait vs Individual Identity

For any group, species, faction, caste, school, clan, or profession:

- Shared group traits may build world texture or prejudice.
- Individual traits identify a person.
- A shared trait cannot be the sole personal identifier unless the text explains why local context narrows it to that person.

First appearances should separate group-readable traits from individual identifiers when that distinction matters to plot or social perception.

## Repetition and Style

Run prose checks after logic checks:

- Exact repeated sentences.
- Repeated 6+ character or word n-grams across nearby chapters.
- Repeated scene beats, reaction beats, gestures, explanations, and hooks.
- Images or metaphors that conflict with project material culture.
- Project-specific forbidden diction.

Intentional echoes are allowed only when they are marked as deliberate in review notes or the chapter plan.
