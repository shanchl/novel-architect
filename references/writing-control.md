# Writing Control

This skill should help sustain a coherent long-form voice across many chapters. Each novel's style rules live only in that novel's `09_writing/` folder.

## Style Files

`style.md`

- narrative person and tense
- prose density
- dialogue ratio
- humor level
- emotional temperature
- literary vs commercial style
- pacing preferences
- genre-specific texture

`writing_rules.md`

- scene construction rules
- POV constraints
- reveal cadence
- combat, romance, suspense, or comedy handling
- chapter ending expectations
- "de-AI" requirements

`forbidden_patterns.md`

- banned phrases
- banned sentence shapes
- overused gestures
- generic transitions
- genre-breaking diction

`vocabulary.md`

- preferred terms
- special names
- faction, ability, or technical vocabulary
- character-specific verbal habits

`prompts.md`

- novel-specific authoring instructions and constraints

## Anti-AI Pattern Checks

Scan drafts for banned or overused expressions, including project-specific entries and common patterns such as:

- "微微一愣"
- "不禁"
- "嘴角勾起"
- "眼中闪过"
- "空气仿佛凝固"
- "倒吸一口凉气"
- "这一刻终于明白"
- "命运的齿轮"

Do not simply delete every occurrence mechanically. Replace with concrete action, sensory detail, subtext, rhythm change, or silence when it improves the scene.

Also check pattern-level issues:

- repeated sentence openings or paragraph rhythms across adjacent paragraphs
- excessive generic emotion labels without concrete behavior
- reflexive reaction beats that do not change the scene
- abstract summary where a scene needs action, dialogue, or sensory grounding
- cliché body language clusters such as eyes, smiles, breath, fists, silence, and frozen air
- repeated explanatory transitions that flatten tension
- every scene ending with the same style of hook
- characters speaking with interchangeable diction

For each detected pattern, cite a short example, classify severity, and propose a scene-specific replacement strategy. Prefer local rewrites over global deletion.

## Chapter Quality Review

Review a chapter for:

- whether the chapter goal was fulfilled
- whether every scene changes conflict, information, relationship, or state
- protagonist agency and motivation
- tension shape and pacing
- clarity of reveals
- emotional continuity
- consistency with character voices
- ratio of exposition, action, dialogue, and interiority
- ending hook strength
- banned patterns and generic phrasing

When revising, preserve canon unless the user requests story changes. If meaning changes, regenerate the chapter summary and state updates.

## Drafting Guidance

Draft from the chapter plan, not from the previous chapter alone. Make each chapter do planned story work: advance conflict, alter states, reveal or conceal information intentionally, and maintain reader momentum.

If target word count is too large for one response, write in coherent scenes and save progress in `06_chapters/drafts/` or continue from a clear section marker.
